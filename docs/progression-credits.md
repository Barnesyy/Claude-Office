# Progression sprites: credits

Every sprite in `public/sprites/progression/` is drawn for this fork by
`scripts/generate-progression-sprites.py` (PIL and numpy: gradient-filled shapes on an
isometric grid, a thin dark outline, a soft floor shadow). No outside art is used.
Licence: MIT, with this repository.

| Sprite | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| trophy-cabinet | display cabinet; its manifest entry says where the trophies, level plate and XP bar go |
| trophy-bronze, -silver, -gold, -platinum | the cups on its shelves |
| mailbox-up, mailbox-down | mailbox, flag up or down |
| shipped-bin, parcel | crate and the parcels piled in it |
| planter, plant-0..3 | window box and its plants |
| lessons-binder | binders on the counter |
| medal, crown, clover, gem, padlock | panel and toast icons, and the rare finds |

The room also reuses upstream's own MIT sprites, unchanged:
`/sprites/rooms/server-rack-2.webp` (backtest bay), `/sprites/decoration/money-tree.png`,
`/sprites/trading/props/equity-curve-frame.png`, `/sprites/trading/props/bull-trophy.png`
(the rare golden bull), `/sprites/effects/star.png` and `/sprites/effects/party.png`
(the star burst when a piece arrives).

No Dunder Mifflin or other third-party trademarked art is used.
